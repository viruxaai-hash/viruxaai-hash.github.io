What Mason33 is

Mason33 is a twelve-year self-directed practice in thirty-three degrees. Each day it names one thing to do, and each degree asks you to write down what it produced. It is a private discipline, kept by you and witnessed by nobody.

Mason33 is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Freemasonry or any Masonic body. The allegory it draws on — the seven liberal arts, the four cardinal virtues, the working tools read as emblem — is public domain and has been for centuries. The emblems and marks of existing organisations are not, and none are used here.

Several degrees touch on the body, on appetite, on grief and on difficult conversations. Use your judgement, and treat every practice as an invitation rather than an instruction. You are responsible for deciding what is safe and appropriate for you, and for seeking qualified help where you need it. If a practice would be harmful to you, skip it — a missed day costs a day and never the path.
